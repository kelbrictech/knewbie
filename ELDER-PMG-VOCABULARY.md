# Elder KNEWBIE — Preach My Gospel Vocabulary Bank

This bank is for the Elder / Missionary English track. Meanings are concise, learner-facing paraphrases aligned to the 2023 *Preach My Gospel* manual and official Church Topics pages. The app should recycle these words across listening, reading, writing, and speaking rather than treating them as a one-time glossary.

## Core recurring vocabulary

- **missionary** — A servant called to invite others to come unto Christ and help them receive the restored gospel.
- **companion** — The missionary assigned to work, study, plan, and teach with you.
- **scripture** — Sacred writings containing God's word, prophetic teachings, and inspired accounts of God's dealings with His children.
- **testimony** — A spiritual witness given by the Holy Ghost.
- **covenant** — A sacred promise with God; God promises blessings as we keep our promises to Him.
- **ordinance** — A sacred act or ceremony performed by priesthood authority.
- **priesthood** — God's power and authority, including authority He gives people to act in His name.
- **repentance** — Turning to God and away from sin so thoughts, desires, and actions become more aligned with His will.
- **Restoration** — The return of the fulness of Jesus Christ's gospel, Church, and priesthood authority through the Prophet Joseph Smith.
- **revelation** — Communication and guidance from God to His children.
- **diligence** — Consistent, valiant effort.
- **discern** — Recognize what is true or needed, especially with careful judgment and guidance of the Spirit.

## Integration rule

The learner should repeatedly **hear, read, say, write, define in plain English, and use** the vocabulary in changed missionary contexts. Core vocabulary should recur across multiple missions; expanded vocabulary is staged where it naturally fits the mission task. Vocabulary exposure is not mastery.
